01 — About

Every build starts with two studs clicking together.

HackIEEE is the flagship hackathon of the IEEE Nirma University Student Branch, run jointly by the Computer Society, the Intelligent Transportation Systems Society and the Signal Processing Society.

You get 36 hours, a workbench and a brief. Mentors circulate through the night, hardware is on the shelf, and everything ends with an eight-minute demo on the main stage.

03 — The Decree

The problems are
not suggestions.

Hell answers to me… for I. AM. DOOM.

Somewhere behind the curtain, a masked head of state has drafted four impossible briefs and signed them all with the same iron hand.

Problem statements drop at kickoff and not one minute earlier. They are deliberately harder than you want them to be — that is the point. You have 36 hours, a table, and no permission to be timid.

  • I. The brief is sealed until the opening ceremony.
  • II. One track per team. Choose it and live with it.
  • III. A demo that does not run is a demo that does not count.
  • IV. Doom does not accept excuses. Neither do the judges.

Late edition · Ahmedabad · Campus bureau

Menace? No — mentors.

Reports of masked figures swinging between tables at 3 a.m. have been confirmed. They are our mentors, and they are here to help.

Mentor rounds run every four hours across all four tracks — systems, security, signals and product. If you are stuck, put the little flag up on your table and someone will drop in.

Pre-event workshops cover the basics for each track, the hardware shelf is open all weekend, and the floor never actually closes. With great uptime comes great responsibility.

07 — FAQ

The manual.

Who can participate?

Any undergraduate or postgraduate student with a valid college ID. You do not need to be an IEEE member, and teams from other institutes are welcome.

What is the team size?

Two to four members. Solo entries are allowed and we will help you find a team at check-in.

Is there a registration fee?

No. Entry is free for shortlisted teams, and meals, workspace and Wi-Fi across the 36 hours are covered.

I have never been to a hackathon. Should I come?

Yes. A large part of the floor will be first-timers, mentors run scheduled rounds through the night, and pre-event workshops cover the basics for each track.

Can we start building beforehand?

Ideation and research are fine, but code must be written after kickoff. Open-source libraries and starters are allowed — just declare them.

Who owns the IP?

You do. Teams keep full ownership of everything they build. We only ask permission to showcase it in IEEE Nirma recaps.
